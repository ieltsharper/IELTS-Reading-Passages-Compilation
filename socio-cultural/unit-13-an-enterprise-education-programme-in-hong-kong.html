An enterprise education programme in Hong Kong

The Teen Entrepreneurs Competition (TEC) is an enterprise education programme developed by the Business Studies Division at the Hong Kong Institute of Education (HKIED) - the major training body for school teachers in Hong Kong. It has the following key objectives: to enable secondary school business students to integrate their conceptual learning with real business practices; to promote a collaborative approach to enterprise education; and to build up formal links between HKIEd, schools and entrepreneurs.

Among the various enterprise programmes in Hong Kong, TEC is unique as it is principally organised by pre-service teachers on their B.Ed course. At the beginning of the academic year, these pre-service teachers form an executive committee. They are advised by tutors in the Business Studies Division and some of them are selected to act as TEC instructors. These instructors attend a series of training sessions that cover the concepts of entrepreneurship and practical skills for business start-ups. They are then sent to the schools to co-work with teachers and meet the competition teams.

Over a three-month period, the teams are required to brainstorm an innovative business start-up that is suitable for the Chinese New Year. They then draft a business proposal, turn this into a real business by sourcing or creating their products, and set up a market stall at the HKIEd campus. The teams' business ideas and sales performance are assessed by an adjudicating panel that is made up of entrepreneurs, school principals, teachers, students and customers.

TEC has run annually for three years and has received favourable responses from both the education and local communities. In a recent evaluation survey, most participants ranked themselves in the satisfactory performance category and considered they had gained practical skills to run a small business. A typical response was: 'I understand enterprise better and have developed a good team spirit. Moreover, positive views were obtained from some participants despite the fact that they had not had a good experience. For example, one participant reflected: 'I initially received a lot of criticism and even thought about giving up. However, I eventually learned to work in a systematic way."

The positive evaluation outcomes came not only from participants but from other stakeholders as well. The adjudicators commented that most of the business plans demonstrated an adequate understanding and application of entrepreneurship in the context of seasonal sales. In addition, the school teachers stated that TEC was a good platform in that it enabled their students to engage in business. They felt the zero drop-out rate strongly supported their views on the promising future of TEC.

The positive post-activity evaluations were indications of the programme's success, but an assessment of how well TEC met its objectives will clarify how sustainable it is as an enterprise education programme. One objective was to provide participant with the opportunity to integrate conceptual learning with real business situations. The research studies that were carried out indicated that the school students taking part in TEC were able to apply entrepreneurial knowledge gained in their studies to the authentic business situations created by the programme. In fact, participants not only succeeded in doing this, but also increased their overall understanding of business enterprise.

However, the financial and manpower support required by this kind of practical experience is beyond the resources of many schools. In fact, most teachers have little choice but to adopt a limited range of teaching methods, mostly based around traditional ones. Moreover, the dominant examination-orientated culture leads teachers to concentrate on written tests rather than explore practical experiences. TEC, however, is able to empower participants to be active learning agents, and provides a feasible means of filling the gap in educational provision.

The research studies also demonstrated that the objective of promoting a collaborative approach to enterprise education was met. From the very start of the programme considerable emphasis was placed on collaboration and teamwork. The participants had to cope in a learning environment which was full of ever-changing challenges, and this meant they had to find ways to deal with events as they occurred. This involved participants taking responsibility for their assigned role within the team and working closely with other team members. Moreover, at certain points, participants had to collaborate with other TEC stakeholders such as entrepreneurs and members of the local community.

The third aim was to develop a network between business and the business education field. Local entrepreneurs acted as sponsors and had an important role in ensuring the programme was financially viable. The network broadly consisted of three tiers. The first tier was made up of key players, including the participants, the teachers, the instructors and the executive committee. There was close collaboration amongst these groups: members of the committee communicated regularly with instructors to disseminate information to participants, and the school teachers and instructors met often to provide suitable guidance to participants. The second tier was an organisational level that mainly consisted of the participating schools and the HKIEd. The contribution of the teacher training institute was to provide logistical support for implementing the programme, including the provision of a venue and a credible point of contact between the schools and the committee. Finally, the third tier consisted of the entrepreneurs who served as judges or sponsors, community groups, the local press and the customers themselves. The positive responses of all these stakeholders and their continuing support is a vote of confidence for the sustainability of TEC in Hong Kong.

(1) The positive (tích cực) post-activity evaluations (đánh giá) were indications of the programme's success, but an assessment (sự đánh giá) of how well TEC met its objectives will clarify how sustainable (bền vững) it is as an enterprise education programme.

→ Xương sống 1: Đánh giá tích cực sau hoạt động là dấu hiệu thành công. Xương sống 2: nhưng đánh giá mức độ TEC đạt mục tiêu mới làm rõ chương trình bền vững đến đâu (câu chuyển ý: từ cảm nhận → mục tiêu).

(2) One objective (mục tiêu) was to provide participant with the opportunity to integrate (tích hợp) conceptual learning with real business situations.

→ Một mục tiêu là giúp người tham gia kết hợp lý thuyết với tình huống kinh doanh thật (= mục tiêu 1 ở Đoạn 1).

(3) The research (nghiên cứu) studies that were carried out indicated (cho thấy) that the school students taking part in TEC were able to apply entrepreneurial knowledge gained in their studies to the authentic business situations created (tạo ra) by the programme.

→ Các nghiên cứu cho thấy học sinh tham gia TEC đã vận dụng được kiến thức khởi nghiệp học trên lớp vào tình huống kinh doanh thật → câu 37.

(4) In fact, participants not only succeeded in doing this, but also increased their overall (tổng thể) understanding of business enterprise.

→ Thực tế, người tham gia không chỉ làm được điều này mà còn nâng cao hiểu biết tổng thể về kinh doanh.

🔗 Mạch đoạn: Chuyển từ “cảm nhận” sang “đánh giá mục tiêu” → mục tiêu 1 (lý thuyết + thực hành) → đạt được, thậm chí vượt mong đợi.

✅ Câu 37 → A (The programme enabled participants to put theory into practice.)

Dẫn chứng: “the school students taking part in TEC were able to apply entrepreneurial knowledge gained in their studies to the authentic business situations”

Giải thích: Vận dụng kiến thức học được vào tình huống thật = đưa lý thuyết vào thực tiễn → A.

Bẫy: B – chỉ nói đánh giá mục tiêu sẽ làm rõ tính bền vững, không nói có bất đồng. D – ngược lại, hiểu biết của họ tăng lên.

ĐOẠN 7

(1) However, the financial (tài chính) and manpower support required (cần có) by this kind of practical experience is beyond the resources (nguồn lực) of many schools.

→ Tuy nhiên, hỗ trợ tài chính và nhân lực mà kiểu trải nghiệm thực tế này cần vượt quá nguồn lực của nhiều trường.

(2) In fact, most teachers have little choice but to adopt a limited range (phạm vi hạn chế) of teaching methods (phương pháp), mostly based around traditional (truyền thống) ones.

→ Thực tế, hầu hết giáo viên không có nhiều lựa chọn ngoài một số ít phương pháp dạy, chủ yếu là truyền thống.

(3) Moreover, the dominant (chi phối) examination-orientated culture (văn hóa) leads teachers to concentrate on written tests rather than explore practical experiences.

→ Hơn nữa, văn hóa nặng thi cử khiến giáo viên tập trung vào bài kiểm tra viết thay vì khám phá trải nghiệm thực tế.

(4) TEC, however, is able to empower participants to be active learning agents, and provides a feasible means of filling the gap in educational provision (cung cấp giáo dục).

→ Tuy vậy, TEC có thể trao quyền cho người tham gia trở thành người học chủ động, và mang lại cách khả thi để lấp khoảng trống trong giáo dục.

🔗 Mạch đoạn: However chỉ ra bối cảnh khó khăn của trường học (thiếu nguồn lực, phương pháp truyền thống, văn hóa thi cử) → however lần 2: TEC chính là lời giải cho khoảng trống đó.

ĐOẠN 8

(1) The research (nghiên cứu) studies also demonstrated (chứng minh) that the objective (mục tiêu) of promoting (thúc đẩy) a collaborative approach (cách tiếp cận) to enterprise education was met.

→ Nghiên cứu cũng chứng minh mục tiêu 2 – thúc đẩy cách tiếp cận hợp tác – đã đạt được.

(2) From the very start of the programme considerable (đáng kể) emphasis (sự nhấn mạnh) was placed on collaboration and teamwork.

→ Ngay từ đầu, sự hợp tác và làm việc nhóm đã được đặc biệt chú trọng.

(3) The participants had to cope in a learning environment (môi trường học tập) which was full of ever-changing challenges (thử thách), and this meant they had to find ways to deal with events as they occurred (xảy ra).

→ Người tham gia phải xoay xở trong môi trường học tập đầy thử thách luôn thay đổi, và điều đó có nghĩa là họ phải tìm cách xử lý sự việc khi chúng xảy ra → câu 38.

(4) This involved (đòi hỏi) participants taking responsibility for their assigned (được giao) role (vai trò) within the team (đội) and working closely with other team members.

→ Điều này đòi hỏi người tham gia chịu trách nhiệm với vai trò được giao và làm việc chặt chẽ với đồng đội.

(5) Moreover, at certain points, participants had to collaborate with other TEC stakeholders such as entrepreneurs and members of the local community (cộng đồng địa phương).

→ Ngoài ra, ở một số thời điểm, họ phải hợp tác với các bên liên quan khác như doanh nhân và cộng đồng.

🔗 Mạch đoạn: Mục tiêu 2 (hợp tác) đã đạt → vì sao: chương trình đặt nặng làm việc nhóm + môi trường đầy thử thách buộc họ phải hợp tác (trong đội và với bên ngoài).

✅ Câu 38 → C (The nature of the situations ensured that participants co-operated.)

Dẫn chứng: “The participants had to cope in a learning environment which was full of ever-changing challenges… This involved participants… working closely with other team members.”

Giải thích: Bản chất tình huống (thử thách liên tục) buộc người tham gia phải hợp tác → C.

Bẫy: B – nghiên cứu chứng minh mục tiêu đạt được, không phải “chưa kết luận”. A, D – không có thông tin.

ĐOẠN 9

(1) The third aim was to develop a network (mạng lưới) between business and the business education field.

→ Mục tiêu thứ ba là phát triển mạng lưới giữa giới kinh doanh và giáo dục kinh doanh.

(2) Local entrepreneurs acted as sponsors and had an important role (vai trò) in ensuring (đảm bảo) the programme was financially (về tài chính) viable.

→ Doanh nhân địa phương đóng vai trò nhà tài trợ và có vai trò quan trọng trong việc đảm bảo chương trình khả thi về tài chính → câu 39.

(3) The network broadly consisted of three tiers.

→ Mạng lưới gồm ba tầng.

(4) The first tier was made up of key players (bên chủ chốt), including the participants, the teachers, the instructors and the executive committee.

→ Tầng 1 gồm các bên chủ chốt: người tham gia, giáo viên, hướng dẫn viên, ban điều hành.

(5) There was close collaboration amongst these groups: members of the committee communicated regularly with instructors to disseminate information to participants, and the school teachers and instructors met often to provide suitable guidance to participants.

→ Có sự hợp tác chặt chẽ: ban điều hành liên lạc thường xuyên với hướng dẫn viên để truyền thông tin, và giáo viên với hướng dẫn viên gặp nhau thường xuyên để hướng dẫn học sinh.

(6) The second tier was an organisational level that mainly consisted of the participating schools and the HKIEd.

→ Tầng 2 là cấp tổ chức, chủ yếu gồm các trường tham gia và HKIEd.

(7) The contribution (đóng góp) of the teacher training institute was to provide logistical support for implementing (triển khai) the programme, including the provision of a venue and a credible point of contact (liên lạc) between the schools and the committee.

→ Đóng góp của viện đào tạo giáo viên (HKIEd) là hỗ trợ hậu cần để triển khai chương trình, gồm cung cấp địa điểm và làm đầu mối liên lạc đáng tin cậy giữa các trường và ban điều hành → câu 40.

(8) Finally, the third tier consisted of the entrepreneurs who served as judges or sponsors, community groups, the local press and the customers themselves.

→ Cuối cùng, tầng 3 gồm doanh nhân (giám khảo, nhà tài trợ), các nhóm cộng đồng, báo chí địa phương và khách hàng.

(9) The positive responses of all these stakeholders and their continuing support is a vote of confidence for the sustainability (tính bền vững) of TEC in Hong Kong.

→ Phản hồi tích cực và sự ủng hộ liên tục của các bên là sự tín nhiệm đối với tính bền vững của TEC. (vote of confidence là nghĩa bóng, không phải HKIEd bỏ phiếu → bẫy phương án A câu 40.)

🔗 Mạch đoạn: Mục tiêu 3 (mạng lưới) → vai trò doanh nhân (tài trợ) → cấu trúc 3 tầng (chủ chốt → tổ chức → cộng đồng) → kết luận: TEC bền vững.

✅ Câu 39 → D (Entrepreneurs helped to make the programme workable in terms of costs.)

Dẫn chứng: “Local entrepreneurs acted as sponsors and had an important role in ensuring the programme was financially viable.”

Giải thích: financially viable = khả thi về chi phí → D. Bẫy: C – địa điểm (venue) do HKIEd cung cấp. A – bài không nói doanh nhân tư vấn chuyên môn.

✅ Câu 40 → D (was a useful link between the other contributors)

Dẫn chứng: “The contribution of the teacher training institute was to provide… a credible point of contact between the schools and the committee.”

Giải thích: point of contact between… = cầu nối hữu ích giữa các bên → D.

Bẫy: A – vote of confidence chỉ là cách nói về sự ủng hộ của các bên, HKIEd không “bỏ phiếu”. B – các nhóm cộng đồng thuộc tầng 3, không nói HKIEd vận động họ.
