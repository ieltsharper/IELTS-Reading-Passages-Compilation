Orientation of birds

A For many of us, the way birds are able to orientate is both astounding and difficult to appreciate fully. For instance, the annual migration of tire golden plover of the Pacific takes it from Alaska to Hawaii on a flight of well over 3,000 kilometres, and if it were to deviate by only one degree, it would miss the island on which it nests.

B The first systematic studies on orientation in birds were made possible by the ‘homing instinct' exhibited by so many species. Birds are caught at a time when they show an attachment to their territory, especially during the nesting season. They are taken to some spot, released, and the percentage of returns is recorded. The distance can be varied, and the direction, as well as the method of transporting them, and then the influence of climatic and other factors on their ability to find their way home can be studied. These experiments have shown a wide variation in ability to home, and three types of homing behaviour have been identified.

C In the first type, birds methodically explore the area in which they are released until they pick up some familiar feature, and then they quickly find their way back to the nest. Such birds possess a highly developed visual memory, as experiments with pigeons have shown. Domestic pigeons have been trained to peck at a certain point on an aerial photograph, with a system of rewards, and four years later the birds were still able to respond to this training when placed on the aerial photograph. Birds' eyes have a power of resolution two to three times greater than ours, enabling them to pick up very fine details. If a bird uses only this type of homing behaviour, however, it can only succeed if the point of release is not too far away. If the birds are transported 800 kilometres from their nest, it is only by good fortune that they find their way back as a result of long exploratory flights. Usually, the area known to a bird is its feeding territory. Released within this area, the birds soon make their return; release them outside it and far fewer return. However, if a bird is released for a second time in the same place, its visual memory comes into play, and the bird, no longer requiring tedious exploratory flights, will return much more quickly.

D The second type of homing behaviour is shown by birds that are capable of choosing their flight direction and holding to it for the rest of their journey. How do they decide what direction to take? They appear to choose their normal migration direction even if they are released in a different place from their usual stalling point. If, for example, birds which normally fly to the north-east to reach latitude 45 degrees north are released at that latitude, they will immediately start flying north-east anyway. So if they're released further to the west, they'll maintain the correct direction, but fly west of their destination, and so fail to arrive.

E The third type of homing behaviour shows the highest degree of orientation. Released at one point, the birds immediately take stock of it, compare its position with that of the nest, decide on the direction and fly off. This happens even if the birds are in a country right off their migration routes, where they have never been before. In one example, a laysan albatross returned to its nesting area on Midway Island in the middle of the Pacific, having flown over 5,000 kilometres from the west coast of the USA in just over ten days. This is a perfect example of the third type of homing, for the albatross clearly couldn't rely on any landmarks over the vast expanse of the Pacific Ocean.

F The percentage of successful birds varies greatly, being highest in those species with a strong migratory behaviour. Thus the lesser black-backed gull is more migratory than the herring gull and more often reaches ‘home'. Great migrants such as the swift have the highest percentage of returns. In one case, seven out of nine alpine swifts were recaptured at their nests after being displaced some 1,400 kilometres; one made the journey in three days.

G What part does heredity play in all this? Two research studies suggest that instinctive, i.e. genetically inherited, behaviour patterns play a part in navigation. The first was carried out by Ernst Schuz and it is highly significant. Schuz caught first year European storks and released them later, after the departure of the adult storks at a time when they normally make their south-west autumn migration to Africa. The recaptures showed that, in spite of the fact that there were no adults to guide them, the birds unanimously headed south-west. This was a most striking finding, for it showed that the birds had an innate and unlearned attraction for the African wintering area that they have occupied for thousands of years.

H The case of starlings is a little different. These birds have a great aptitude for homing, but this behaviour differs between age groups. Birds that were shifted to the south-east of their normal migration route split into two lots. The adults, in full possession of their gift for orientation, found their wintering area by modifying their direction by 90 degrees, whereas the juveniles sought their winter quarters to the south-east of their real position.

Xương sống: They | appear → chúng có vẻ chọn hướng di cư quen thuộc dù được thả ở nơi khác điểm xuất phát thường lệ

Ý phụ: their normal migration direction → hướng di cư quen thuộc (Q15)

Ý phụ: even if … released in a different place from their usual starting point → dù được thả ở nơi khác điểm xuất phát thường lệ

💡 Mẹo: “normal” = “accustomed” trong đề.

(4) If, for example, birds which normally (bình thường) fly to the north-east to reach latitude 45 degrees north are released (thải ra) at that latitude, they will immediately start flying north-east anyway.

→ Ví dụ chim thường bay về đông bắc để tới vĩ độ 45°B, nếu được thả ở vĩ độ đó vẫn bay đông bắc ngay.

(5) So if they're released (thải ra) further to the west, they'll maintain the correct direction, but fly west of their destination, and so fail to arrive.

Xương sống: they | 'll maintain + fly + fail → nếu thả xa hơn về phía tây, chúng giữ đúng hướng nhưng bay lệch phía tây điểm đến nên không tới được

Ý phụ: but fly west of their destination, and so fail to arrive → nhưng bay lệch về phía tây điểm đến nên không tới được

💡 Mẹo: “destination” → Q16.

🔗 Mạch đoạn: Kiểu 2: chọn và giữ hướng di cư quen thuộc bất kể nơi thả; nếu thả lệch về tây thì bay lệch điểm đến.

✅ Câu 15 → migration direction (Second type: birds select their accustomed ___, no matter where they are released)

Dẫn chứng: “They appear to choose their normal migration direction even if they are released in a different place”

Giải thích: “accustomed” ↔ “normal”; “no matter where released” ↔ “even if released in a different place”.

✅ Câu 16 → destination (As a result, they may miss their ___)

Dẫn chứng: “fly west of their destination, and so fail to arrive”

Giải thích: “miss” ↔ “fail to arrive”.

ĐOẠN E

(1) The third type of homing behaviour shows the highest degree of orientation (định hướng).

→ Kiểu thứ ba thể hiện mức độ định hướng cao nhất.

(2) Released (thải ra) at one point, the birds immediately take stock of it, compare its position (vị trí) with that of the nest, decide on the direction and fly off.

Xương sống: the birds | take + compare + decide + fly → được thả ở một điểm, chim lập tức đánh giá vị trí, so với vị trí tổ, quyết định hướng và bay đi

Ý phụ: Released at one point → (phân từ) khi được thả ở một điểm

Ý phụ: compare its position with that of the nest → so vị trí đó với vị trí của tổ (that = position)

💡 Mẹo: 4 động từ song song cùng chủ ngữ “the birds”.

(3) This happens even if the birds are in a country right off their migration (di cư) routes (tuyến đường), where they have never been before.

→ Điều này xảy ra ngay cả khi chim ở vùng nằm ngoài đường di cư, nơi chúng chưa từng đến.

(4) In one example, a laysan albatross returned to its nesting area (khu vực) on Midway Island in the middle of the Pacific, having flown over 5,000 kilometres from the west coast of the USA in just over ten days.

Xương sống: a laysan albatross | returned → ví dụ: một con hải âu laysan quay về vùng làm tổ trên đảo Midway sau khi bay hơn 5.000 km từ bờ tây nước Mỹ chỉ trong hơn mười ngày

Ý phụ: having flown over 5,000 kilometres … in just over ten days → (phân từ hoàn thành) sau khi bay hơn 5.000 km trong hơn mười ngày

💡 Mẹo: Đáp án Q18: laysan albatross.

(5) This is a perfect example of the third type of homing, for the albatross clearly couldn't rely (sự phụ thuộc) on any landmarks over the vast expanse of the Pacific Ocean.

Xương sống: This + the albatross | is + couldn't rely → đây là ví dụ hoàn hảo của kiểu thứ ba vì hải âu rõ ràng không thể dựa vào mốc nào trên đại dương mênh mông

Ý phụ: for the albatross clearly couldn't rely on any landmarks … → vì hải âu rõ ràng không thể dựa vào mốc nào

💡 Mẹo: “for” = vì (liên từ); “landmarks” → Q17.

🔗 Mạch đoạn: Kiểu 3: định hướng cao nhất — chim tự đánh giá vị trí rồi bay thẳng; ví dụ hải âu bay hơn 5.000 km không mốc.

✅ Câu 17 → landmarks (Third type: no ___ to make use of)

Dẫn chứng: “the albatross clearly couldn't rely on any landmarks over the vast expanse of the Pacific Ocean”

Giải thích: “no ___ to make use of” ↔ “couldn't rely on any landmarks”.

✅ Câu 18 → (laysan) albatross (One bird with this type of skill is the ___)

Dẫn chứng: “a laysan albatross returned to its nesting area … a perfect example of the third type of homing”

Giải thích: ví dụ của kiểu 3 = laysan albatross.

ĐOẠN F

(1) The percentage of successful (thành công) birds varies greatly (thay đổi rất nhiều), being highest in those species with a strong migratory (di cư) behaviour.

→ Tỷ lệ chim thành công thay đổi lớn, cao nhất ở các loài di cư mạnh.

(2) Thus the lesser black-backed gull is more migratory (di cư) than the herring gull and more often reaches ‘home'.

→ Vì vậy mòng biển lưng đen nhỏ di cư mạnh hơn mòng biển bạc và thường về “nhà” hơn.

(3) Great migrants such as the swift have the highest percentage of returns.

→ Những loài di cư giỏi như én có tỷ lệ quay về cao nhất.

(4) In one case, seven out of nine alpine swifts were recaptured at their nests after being displaced (dời chỗ) some 1,400 kilometres; one made the journey in three days.

→ Một trường hợp: bảy trên chín con én núi bị đem đi xa khoảng 1.400 km được bắt lại ở tổ; một con mất ba ngày.

🔗 Mạch đoạn: Tỷ lệ về nhà cao nhất ở loài di cư mạnh (én); ví dụ én núi bị dời 1.400 km.

✅ Câu 24 → F (Alpine swift → a high success rate in finding nests)

Dẫn chứng: “seven out of nine alpine swifts were recaptured at their nests after being displaced some 1,400 kilometres”

Giải thích: 7/9 con về được tổ = tỉ lệ thành công cao.

Bẫy: E (strength to fly far) không được nêu; bài nói về tỉ lệ thành công, không nói sức bền.

ĐOẠN G

(1) What part does heredity play in all this?

→ Di truyền đóng vai trò gì trong tất cả điều này?

(2) Two research (nghiên cứu) studies suggest that instinctive, i.e. genetically inherited, behaviour patterns play a part in navigation.

→ Hai nghiên cứu cho thấy các mẫu hành vi bản năng, tức di truyền, đóng vai trò trong điều hướng.

(3) The first was carried out by Ernst Schuz and it is highly significant (rất quan trọng).

→ Nghiên cứu đầu tiên do Ernst Schuz thực hiện và rất quan trọng.

Xương sống: Schuz | caught + released → Schuz bắt cò châu Âu năm đầu và thả chúng muộn hơn, sau khi cò trưởng thành đã bay đi, vào lúc chúng thường di cư mùa thu về châu Phi

Ý phụ: released them later, after the departure of the adult storks → thả chúng muộn hơn, sau khi cò trưởng thành đã đi

Ý phụ: at a time when they normally make their south-west autumn migration to Africa → vào lúc chúng thường di cư mùa thu về Phi

💡 Mẹo: Hoãn thả cò non → Q22 (migration delayed for experimental purposes).

(5) The recaptures showed that, in spite of the fact that there were no adults (trưởng thành) to guide them, the birds unanimously headed south-west.

Xương sống: The recaptures + the birds | showed + headed → những con bắt lại cho thấy dù không có chim lớn dẫn đường, chúng đồng loạt bay về tây nam

Ý phụ: in spite of the fact that there were no adults to guide them → dù không có chim trưởng thành dẫn đường

💡 Mẹo: “unanimously” = đồng loạt.

(6) This was a most striking finding, for it showed that the birds had an innate and unlearned attraction for the African wintering area (khu vực) that they have occupied for thousands of years.

Xương sống: This + it | was + showed → đây là phát hiện nổi bật vì nó cho thấy chim có sức hút bẩm sinh, không học, với vùng trú đông châu Phi mà chúng sống hàng nghìn năm

Ý phụ: an innate and unlearned attraction for the African wintering area → sức hút bẩm sinh, không học, với vùng trú đông châu Phi

Ý phụ: that they have occupied for thousands of years → (MĐQH) mà chúng cư trú hàng nghìn năm

💡 Mẹo: “for” = vì.

🔗 Mạch đoạn: Di truyền có vai trò: thí nghiệm của Schuz thả cò non muộn, không có chim lớn dẫn đường vẫn bay đúng hướng.
