Eco-Resort Management Practices

A Ecotourism is often regarded as a form of nature-based tourism and has become an important alternative source of tourists. In addition to providing the traditional resort-leisure product, it has been argued that ecotourism resort management, an educational and interpretive component, and direct and indirect contributions to the conservation of the natural and cultural environment (Ayala, 1996).

B Couran Cove Island Resort is a large integrated ecotourism-based resort located south of Brisbane on the Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia. As the world's population becomes increasingly urbanized, the demand for tourist attractions which are environmentally friendly, serene and offer amenities of a unique nature, has grown rapidly. Couran Cove Resort, which is one such tourist attractions, is located on South Stradbroke Island, occupying approximately 150 hectares of the island. South Stradbroke Island is separated from the mainland by the Broadwater, a stretch of sea 3 kilometers wide more than a century ago, there was only one Stradbroke Island, and there were at least four aboriginal tribes living and hunting on the island. Regrettably, most of the original island dwellers were eventually killed by diseases such as tuberculosis, smallpox and influenza by the end of the 19th century. The second ship wreak on the island in 1894, and subsequent destruction of the ship (the Cambus Wallace) because it contained dynamite, caused a large crater in the sandhills on Stradbroke Island. Eventually, the ocean broke through the weakened land form and Stradbroke became two islands. Couran Cove Island Resort is built on one of the world's few naturally-occurring sand lands, which is home to a wide range of plant communities and one of the largest remaining remnants of the rare livistona rainforest left on the Gold Coast. Many mangrove and rainforest areas, and Melaleuca Wetlands on South Stradbroke Island (and in Queensland), have been cleared, drained or filled for residential, industrial, agricultural or urban development in the first half of the 20th century. Farmers and graziers finally abandoned South Stradbroke Island in 1939 because the vegetation and the soil conditions there were not suitable for agricultural activities.

SUSTAINABLE PRACTICES OF COURAN COVE RESORT

Being located on an offshore island, the resort is only accessible by means of water transportation. The resort provides hourly ferry service from the marina on the mainland to and from the island. Within the resort, transport modes include walking trails, bicycle tracks and the beach train. The reception area is the counter of the shop which has not changed in 8 years at least. The accommodation is an octagonal "Bure". These are large rooms that are clean but! The equipment is tired and in some cases just working. Our ceiling fan only worked on high speed for example. Beds are hard but clean, there is television, radio, and old air conditioner and a small fridge. These "Bures" are right on top of each other and night noises do carry so be careful what you say and do. The only thing is the mosquitos but if you forget to bring mosquito repellant they sell some on the island.

As an ecotourism-based resort, most of the planning and development of the attraction has been concentrated on the need to co-exist with the fragile natural environment of South Stradbroke Island to achieve sustainable development.

WATER AND ENERGY MANAGEMENT

C South Stradbroke Island has groundwater at the centre of the island, which has a maximum height of 3 metres above sea level. The water supply is recharged by rainfall and is commonly known as an unconfined freshwater aquifer. Couran Cove Island Resort obtains its water supply by tapping into this aquifer and extracting it via a bore system. Some of the problems which have threatened the island's freshwater supply include pollution, contamination and over-consumption. In order to minimise some of these problems, all laundry activities are carried out on the mainland. The resort considers washing machines as onerous to the island's freshwater supply, and that the detergents contain a high level of phosphates which are a major source of water pollution. The resort uses LPG-power generation rather than a diesel-powered plant for its energy supply, supplemented by wind turbine, which has reduced greenhouse emission by 70% of diesel-equivalent generation methods. Excess heat recovered from the generator is used to heat the swimming pool. Hot water in the eco-cabins and for some of the resort's vehicles are solar-powered. Water efficient fittings are also installed in showers and toilets. However, not all the appliances used by the resort are energy efficient, such as refrigerators. Visitors who stay at the resort are encouraged to monitor their water and energy usage via the in-house television systems, and are rewarded with prizes (such as a free return trip to the resort) accordingly if their usage level is low.

CONCLUDING REMARKS

D We examined a case study of good management practice and a pro-active sustainable tourism stance of an eco-resort. In three years of operation, Couran Cove Island Resort has won 23 international and national awards, including the 2001 Australian Tourism Award in the 4-Star Accommodation category. The resort has embraced and has effectively implemented contemporary environmental management practices. It has been argued that the successful implementation of the principles of sustainability should promote long-term social, economic and environmental benefits, while ensuring and enhancing the prospects of continued viability for the tourism enterprise. Couran Cove Island Resort does not conform to the characteristics of the Resort Development Spectrum, as proposed by Prideaux (2000). According to Prideaux, the resort should be at least at Phase 3 of the model (the National tourism phase), which describes an integrated resort providing 3-4 star hotel-type accommodation. The primary tourist market in Phase 3 of the model consists mainly of interstate visitors. However, the number of interstate and international tourists visiting the resort is small, with the principal visitor markets comprising locals and residents from nearby towns and the Gold Coast region. The carrying capacity of Couran Cove does not seem to be of any concern to the Resort management. Given that it is a private, regulating the number of visitors to the resort to minimize damage done to the natural environment on South Stradbroke Island is not a binding constraint. However, the Resort's growth will eventually be constrained by its carrying capacity, and quantity control should be incorporated in the management strategy of the resort.

Giải thích: barely working = just working; overhead = ceiling → ceiling fan (đáp án chính thức cũng chấp nhận fan).

✅ Câu 9 → air conditioner (There is television, radio, an old ⟦9⟧ and a small fridge.)

Dẫn chứng: “there is television, radio, and old air conditioner and a small fridge”

Giải thích: sao chép nguyên danh sách → air conditioner (hoặc air-conditioner).

✅ Câu 10 → mosquitos (you can buy the repellant for ⟦10⟧ if you forget to bring some.)

Dẫn chứng: “if you forget to bring mosquito repellant they sell some on the island”

Giải thích: buy = sell; repellant for ⟦10⟧ = mosquito repellant → mosquito(s).

Tiến độ sau đoạn không chữ 1: Xong câu 1, 6–10. Còn: 2–5, 11–13.

ĐOẠN KHÔNG CHỮ 2 (trước WATER AND ENERGY MANAGEMENT)

(1) As an ecotourism-based resort, most of the planning and development of the attraction has been concentrated on the need to co-exist with the fragile natural environment (môi trường) of South Stradbroke Island to achieve (đạt được) sustainable (bền vững) development.

→ Xương sống: Phần lớn việc quy hoạch và phát triển tập trung vào việc chung sống với môi trường mong manh của đảo để đạt phát triển bền vững.

🔗 Mạch đoạn: Dẫn nhập: mục tiêu của resort là phát triển bền vững.

Tiến độ sau đoạn không chữ 2: Không có câu mới. Còn: 2–5, 11–13.

ĐOẠN C (dưới tiêu đề WATER AND ENERGY MANAGEMENT)

(1) South Stradbroke Island has groundwater at the centre of the island, which has a maximum (tối đa) height of 3 metres above sea level.

→ Xương sống: Đảo có nước ngầm ở trung tâm, cao tối đa 3 m so với mực nước biển.

(2) The water supply is recharged by rainfall and is commonly known as an unconfined freshwater aquifer.

→ Xương sống: Nguồn nước được bổ sung bởi mưa và gọi là tầng nước ngầm nước ngọt.

💡 rainfall chỉ là cách bổ sung tầng nước ngầm, chưa phải cách resort lấy nước → bẫy của câu 3 (B).

(3) Couran Cove Island Resort obtains its water supply by tapping into this aquifer and extracting it via a bore system.

→ Xương sống: Resort lấy nước bằng cách khai thác tầng nước ngầm qua hệ thống giếng khoan → câu 3.

(4) Some of the problems which have threatened the island's freshwater supply include pollution, contamination and over-consumption.

→ Xương sống: Các vấn đề đe doạ nguồn nước ngọt gồm ô nhiễm, nhiễm bẩn và dùng quá mức.

(5) In order to minimise some of these problems, all laundry activities are carried out on the mainland.

→ Xương sống: Để giảm các vấn đề trên, mọi việc giặt giũ được làm trên đất liền → câu 2.

(6) The resort considers washing machines as onerous to the island's freshwater supply, and that the detergents contain a high level of phosphates which are a major (chính) source (nguồn) of water pollution.

→ Xương sống: Resort coi máy giặt là gánh nặng cho nguồn nước và chất tẩy chứa nhiều phosphate, nguồn ô nhiễm lớn → giải thích lý do của câu 5 trên.

💡 In order to minimise some of these problems nói đến mọi vấn đề (ô nhiễm, nhiễm bẩn, dùng quá mức), nên đáp án rộng “preserve the water and anti-pollution”, không phải chỉ “phosphates”.

(7) The resort uses LPG-power generation rather than a diesel-powered plant for its energy (năng lượng) supply, supplemented by wind turbine, which has reduced greenhouse emission by 70% of diesel-equivalent (tương đương) generation methods.

→ Xương sống: Resort dùng điện chạy khí LPG (bổ sung bằng tuabin gió) thay cho diesel, giảm 70% khí nhà kính.

(8) Excess heat recovered from the generator is used to heat the swimming pool.

→ Xương sống: Nhiệt dư từ máy phát được dùng để làm nóng hồ bơi.

(9) Hot water in the eco-cabins and for some of the resort's vehicles are solar-powered.

→ Xương sống: Nước nóng trong các cabin sinh thái và cho một số xe dùng năng lượng mặt trời → câu 4.

(10) Water efficient fittings are also installed in showers and toilets.

→ Vòi sen, bồn cầu cũng lắp thiết bị tiết kiệm nước.

(11) However, not all the appliances used by the resort are energy (năng lượng) efficient, such as refrigerators.

→ Xương sống: Tuy nhiên không phải thiết bị nào cũng tiết kiệm điện (ví dụ tủ lạnh).

(12) Visitors who stay at the resort are encouraged to monitor their water and energy usage via the in-house television systems, and are rewarded with prizes accordingly if their usage level is low.

→ Xương sống: Khách được khuyến khích theo dõi mức dùng nước/điện qua tivi và được thưởng nếu dùng ít.

🔗 Mạch đoạn: Nước ngầm → khoan lấy nước → giặt trên đất liền → điện LPG + gió → nhiệt dư cho hồ bơi, nước nóng mặt trời → thiết bị chưa đều tiết kiệm → thưởng khách tiết kiệm.

✅ Câu 3 → D (What is the major water supplier in South Stradbroke Island is by …)

Dẫn chứng: “Couran Cove Island Resort obtains its water supply by tapping into this aquifer and extracting it via a bore system.”

Giải thích: boring ground water = extracting (groundwater) via a bore system → D.

Bẫy: B rainfall chỉ bổ sung tầng nước ngầm (“is recharged by rainfall”). A, C không được nhắc.

✅ Câu 2 → B (Why are laundry activities for the resort carried out on the mainland.)

Dẫn chứng: “In order to minimise some of these problems [pollution, contamination and over-consumption], all laundry activities are carried out on the mainland.”

Giải thích: preserve the water and anti-pollution = giảm ô nhiễm, nhiễm bẩn và dùng quá mức nước ngọt → B.

Bẫy: D reduce phosphates – phosphate chỉ là một nguồn ô nhiễm; lý do đầy đủ là giữ nước sạch. A là cách lấy nước, C (tiết kiệm chi phí) không có.

✅ Câu 4 → D (What is applied for heating water on Couran Cove Island Resort)

Dẫn chứng: “Hot water in the eco-cabins and for some of the resort's vehicles are solar-powered.”

Giải thích: heating water = hot water → D (solar).

Bẫy: ⚠ A – LPG chỉ dùng để phát điện; nhiệt dư của máy phát dùng làm nóng hồ bơi, không phải nước nóng cho cabin. B diesel bị thay thế. C gió bổ sung điện. Câu có chút mơ hồ (hồ bơi cũng là “nước”) nhưng đáp án chính thức D phù hợp nhất.

Tiến độ sau Đoạn C: Xong câu 1–4, 6–10. Còn: 5, 11–13 (đều ở đoạn cuối).

ĐOẠN D (CONCLUDING REMARKS)

(1) We examined a case study of good management practice and a pro-active sustainable (bền vững) tourism stance of an eco-resort.

→ Xương sống: Chúng tôi xem xét một ca nghiên cứu về quản lý tốt và lập trường du lịch bền vững chủ động của một eco-resort.

(2) In three years of operation, Couran Cove Island Resort has won 23 international and national awards, including the 2001 Australian Tourism Award in the 4-Star Accommodation category.

→ Xương sống: Trong 3 năm hoạt động, resort đã giành 23 giải quốc tế và quốc gia → bẫy câu 5 (A: giải thưởng là quá khứ).

(3) The resort has embraced and has effectively implemented contemporary environmental management practices.

→ Xương sống: Resort đã đón nhận và triển khai hiệu quả các thực hành quản lý môi trường hiện đại → liên quan lựa chọn A (câu 11–13).

(4) It has been argued that the successful implementation (sự triển khai) of the principles (nguyên tắc) of sustainability should promote long-term social, economic and environmental benefits, while ensuring and enhancing the prospects of continued viability for the tourism enterprise.

→ Xương sống: Người ta cho rằng việc triển khai thành công các nguyên tắc bền vững nên thúc đẩy lợi ích dài hạn về xã hội, kinh tế, môi trường → câu 5 (sustainable … long run).

💡 Chủ đề: sustainability + long-term, nên B (sustainable administration and development in a long run) đúng hơn C (chỉ nhắc “economic and environmental”, thiếu long-term và sustainability).

(5) Couran Cove Island Resort does not conform to the characteristics of the Resort Development Spectrum, as proposed by Prideaux (2000).

→ Xương sống: Resort không phù hợp với đặc điểm của Phổ phát triển resort (Prideaux) → loại lựa chọn B (11–13).

(6) According to Prideaux, the resort should be at least at Phase 3 of the model (the National tourism phase), which describes an integrated resort providing 3-4 star hotel-type accommodation.

→ Xương sống: Theo Prideaux, resort nên ở ít nhất Giai đoạn 3 (resort tích hợp, chỗ ở 3–4 sao) → ý ẩn: hiện chưa đạt tiêu chuẩn này → lựa chọn C.

(7) The primary tourist market in Phase 3 of the model consists mainly of interstate visitors.

→ Xương sống: Thị trường chính của Giai đoạn 3 chủ yếu là khách liên bang.

(8) However, the number of interstate and international tourists visiting the resort is small, with the principal visitor markets comprising locals and residents from nearby towns and the Gold Coast region.

→ Xương sống: Tuy nhiên, số khách liên bang và quốc tế ít; nhóm khách chính là người địa phương và cư dân vùng lân cận → loại lựa chọn D (11–13).

(9) The carrying capacity of Couran Cove does not seem to be of any concern to the Resort management.

→ Xương sống: Sức chứa của resort dường như không phải mối lo của ban quản lý.

(10) Given that it is a private, regulating the number of visitors to the resort to minimize damage done to the natural environment on South Stradbroke Island is not a binding constraint.

→ Xương sống: Vì là cơ sở tư nhân, việc hạn chế số khách để giảm hại cho môi trường không phải ràng buộc bắt buộc. (“a private” thiếu danh từ trong bản gốc.)

(11) However, the Resort's growth will eventually be constrained by its carrying capacity, and quantity control should be incorporated in the management strategy of the resort.

→ Xương sống: Tuy nhiên, sự tăng trưởng sẽ bị giới hạn bởi sức chứa, nên cần đưa kiểm soát số lượng vào chiến lược quản lý → lựa chọn E.

💡 However lật lại ý trước: dù hiện chưa lo, sức chứa cuối cùng sẽ giới hạn mở rộng.

🔗 Mạch đoạn: Ca nghiên cứu → giải thưởng → thực hành môi trường → nguyên tắc bền vững, lợi ích dài hạn → chưa đạt Phase 3 → khách chủ yếu là người địa phương → sức chứa sẽ hạn chế tăng trưởng.

✅ Câu 11–13 → A, C, E (What is true as to the contemporary situation of Couran Cove Island Resort in the last paragraph?)
