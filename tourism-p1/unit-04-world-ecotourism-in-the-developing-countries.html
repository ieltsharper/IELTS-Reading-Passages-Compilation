World Ecotourism in the Developing Countries

A The Ecotourism Society defines ecotourism as "a responsible travel to natural areas which conserves the environment and improves the welfare of local people". It is recognised as being particularly conducive to enriching and enhancing the standing of tourism, on the basis that this form of tourism respects the natural heritage and local populations and are in keeping with the carrying capacity of the sites.

Cuba

B Cuba is undoubtedly an obvious site for ecotourism, with its picturesque beaches, underwater beauty, countryside landscapes, and ecological reserves. An educated population and improved infrastructure of roads and communications adds to the mix. In the Caribbean region, Cuba is now the second most popular tourist destination.

Ecotourism is also seen as an environmental education opportunity to heighten both visitors' and residents' awareness of environmental and conservation issues, and even to inspire conservation action.

Ecotourism has also been credited with promoting peace, by providing opportunities for educational and cultural exchange. Tourists' safety and health are guaranteed.

Raul Castro, brother of the Cuban president, started this initiative to rescue the Cuban tradition of herbal medicine and provide natural medicines for its healthcare system. The school at Las Terrazas Eco-Tourism Community teaches herbal healthcare and children learn not only how to use medicinal herbs, but also to grow them in the school garden for teas, tinctures, ointments and creams.

In Cuba, ecotourism has the potential to alleviate poverty by bringing money into the economy and creating jobs. In addition to the environmental impacts of these efforts, the area works on developing community employment opportunities for locals, in conjunction with ecotourism.

The market segment for Indonesian ecotourism consists of: (i) "The silent generation", 55-64 year-old people who are wealthy enough, generally well-educated and have no dependent children, and can travel for four weeks; (ii) "The baby boom generation", junior successful executives aged 35-54 years, who are likely to be travelling with their family and children (spending 2-3 weeks on travel) - travelling for them is a stress reliever; and (iii) the "X generation", aged 18-29 years, who love to do ecotours as backpackers - they are generally students who can travel for 3-12 months with monthly expenditure of US$300-500. Bẫy: indigenous peoples là đối tượng bị ảnh hưởng, không phải thứ bị phá huỷ.

Tiến độ sau Đoạn D: Xong câu 1, 3, 5, 9–12. Còn: 2, 4, 6–8, 13.

ĐOẠN E (EAST AFRICA)

(1) In East Africa, significantly reducing such illegal hunting and allowing wildlife populations to recover would allow the generation of significant economic benefits (lợi ích kinh tế) through trophy hunting and potentially ecotourism.

→ Xương sống: Ở Đông Phi, giảm mạnh nạn săn bắn trái phép và để quần thể thú hồi phục sẽ tạo lợi ích kinh tế qua săn thú lấy cúp và ecotourism → câu 4, 13.

💡 “such illegal hunting” không có câu nào nhắc trước trong bài (bản gốc bị cắt); vẫn hiểu được từ chính nó.

(2) "Illegal hunting is an extremely inefficient use of wildlife resources (tài nguyên) because it fails to capture the value of wildlife achievable through alternative forms (hình thức thay thế) of use such as trophy hunting and ecotourism," said Peter Lindsey, author (tác giả) of the new study.

→ Xương sống: Lindsey nói săn trái phép kém hiệu quả vì không nắm bắt được giá trị của động vật hoang dã qua các hình thức khác như ecotourism.

(3) Most residents believed that ecotourism could solve this circumstance.

→ Xương sống: Đa số cư dân tin ecotourism có thể giải quyết tình trạng này.

(4) They have passion for local community (cộng đồng) empowerment, loves photography and writes to laud current local conservation efforts, create environmental awareness and promote ecotourism.

→ Họ nhiệt huyết với việc trao quyền cho cộng đồng, viết bài ca ngợi nỗ lực bảo tồn, tạo nhận thức môi trường và quảng bá ecotourism (chia động từ sai trong bản gốc).

🔗 Mạch đoạn: Đông Phi: giảm săn trộm + ecotourism → lợi ích kinh tế; cư dân tin ecotourism giải quyết được.

✅ Câu 4 → B (a place taking a method to stop unlawful poaching)

Dẫn chứng: “In East Africa, significantly reducing such illegal hunting … would allow … ecotourism.”

Giải thích: stop unlawful poaching = reducing illegal hunting → East Africa (B).

✅ Câu 13 → illegal hunting (East Africa also encourages this kind of tourism for cutting the ⟦13⟧ in order to save wild animals.)

Dẫn chứng: “significantly reducing such illegal hunting and allowing wildlife populations to recover”

Giải thích: cutting = reducing; save wild animals = allowing wildlife populations to recover → illegal hunting (2 từ).

Bẫy: ⚠ Khác biệt với đáp án chính thức: bảng đáp án ghi “illegal killing (key) / illegal hunting (passage)”. Cụm illegal killing không xuất hiện trong bài; vì đề yêu cầu từ trong bài nên chọn illegal hunting. Giáo viên cần xác nhận.

Tiến độ sau Đoạn E: Xong câu 1, 3–5, 9–13. Còn: 2, 6–8.

ĐOẠN F (INDONESIA)

(1) In Indonesia, ecotourism started to become an important concept (khái niệm) from 1995, in order to strengthen the domestic (trong nước) travelling movement, the local government (chính quyền địa phương) targeting the right markets is a prerequisite for successful ecotourism.

→ Xương sống: Ở Indonesia ecotourism trở thành khái niệm quan trọng từ 1995; việc chính quyền nhắm đúng thị trường là điều kiện tiên quyết.

(2) The market segment for Indonesian ecotourism consists of: (i) "The silent generation", 55-64 year-old people who are wealthy enough, generally well-educated and have no dependent children, and can travel for four weeks; (ii) "The baby boom generation", junior successful executives aged 35-54 years, who are likely to be travelling with their family and children (spending 2-3 weeks on travel) - travelling for them is a stress (căng thẳng) reliever; and (iii) the "X generation", aged 18-29 years, who love to do ecotours as backpackers - they are generally students who can travel for 3-12 months with monthly expenditure of US$300-500.

→ Xương sống: Thị trường gồm ba nhóm: người 55–64 tuổi khá giả; giám đốc trẻ 35–54 tuổi đi cùng gia đình; thế hệ X 18–29 tuổi, sinh viên đi bụi, chi 300–500 USD/tháng → câu 2 (rich and poor).

(3) It is suggested that promotion of Indonesian ecotourism products should aim to reach these various cohorts of tourists.

→ Nên quảng bá sản phẩm để tiếp cận các nhóm khách đa dạng.

(4) The country welcomes diverse levels of travelers.

→ Xương sống: Đất nước chào đón nhiều tầng lớp du khách → câu 2.

🔗 Mạch đoạn: Indonesia: chính quyền nhắm thị trường → ba nhóm tuổi/thu nhập → đón mọi tầng lớp.

✅ Câu 2 → D (a place suitable for both rich and poor travelers)

Dẫn chứng: “people who are wealthy enough”; “students … monthly expenditure of US$300-500”; “The country welcomes diverse levels of travelers.”

Giải thích: both rich and poor travelers = khách khá giả đến sinh viên chi tiêu thấp, diverse levels → Indonesia (D).

Bẫy: bài không dùng từ “poor”; ý được suy ra từ nhóm sinh viên đi bụi (chi tiêu thấp) và câu chốt.

Tiến độ sau Đoạn F: Xong câu 1–5, 9–13. Còn: 6–8.
